## PR body
```markdown
Stacked on #41710.

## Motivation

With #41710 and an empty Triton cache, Wan2.2-TI2V-5B's first 720p request still decoded 0.17 s slower than later requests. Two costs remained in the VAE decode, and server warmup cannot absorb either of them:

- **Two DupUp3D-add variants compile during the decode.** #41710 unspecialized the sizes but kept every stride of `_dup_up3d_add_kernel`. In the channels-first arms those strides are products of the resolution (`C*H*W`, `W`, ...), so their divisibility class changes with the shape. Two launches landed in a class warmup had not compiled.
- **The lossless norm+SiLU fusion re-verifies itself at every new resolution.** `FusedWanRMSNormSiLU` keyed its first-sight exactness check on the activation's shape and strides. A new resolution therefore re-ran the eager reference chain plus a `torch.equal` host sync 46 times: 46 `cudaStreamSynchronize` calls in the first decode, none in the second.

Warmup cannot cover either: for TI2V, `InputValidationStage` derives the output size from the condition image's aspect ratio. The 64x64 warmup image gives 928x960, while the nightly's 1024x704 photo gives 1152x768, and no fixed warmup image covers every aspect ratio.

## Modifications

- **`_dup_up3d_add_kernel`.** Plain `do_not_specialize` on these strides would make the kernel up to 98% slower: their divisibility is what lets the compiler emit one 128-bit access per four outputs. The wrapper instead derives a vector width `VEC` from the layout. It passes the non-unit strides of `main` and `out`, and `out_w` when W is innermost, divided by `VEC`; the kernel multiplies them back by the constexpr, which restates the same contiguity without depending on their values. Unit strides stay specialized, but not on alignment.
- **`_cat_pad_cl3d_kernel`.** A second instance with the input strides unspecialized serves channels-first inputs, whose loads gather along the channel stride whatever its value. Channels-last inputs keep the fully specialized kernel; unspecializing it there costs 10-12%. The per-layer `pad`/`cache_t` specialization from #41710 stays.
- **`FusedWanRMSNormSiLU`.** The exactness check is keyed on `(device, dtype, x.is_contiguous(), gamma.dtype)`, which is exactly what selects the kernel template. The per-element ops do not depend on the sizes.

## Accuracy Tests

- `test_shape_specialization.py` adds four launches: `wan_cat_pad_channels_first`, and `wan_dup_up3d` in the cf/cf, cf/cl and cl/cf layouts. All four fail on #41710 and pass with this change.
- New `test_first_sight_check_runs_once_per_layout` in `test_wan_norm_silu_post.py`: #41710 verifies 6 signatures (`6 != 2`), this change verifies 2.
- The bitwise cat/pad and DupUp3D tests in `test_layout.py` now sweep channels-last and channels-first arms (four layout pairs for DupUp3D), plus two widths that exercise vector widths 2 and 1.
- `test_layout.py`, `test_shape_specialization.py`, `test_wan_norm_silu_post.py` and `test_model_fast_paths.py`: 286 passed, 1 skipped.
```
