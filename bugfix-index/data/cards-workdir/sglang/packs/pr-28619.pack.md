## Motivation

Fixes #27744



  This is a small patch to fix the Qwen MoE precision issue when pipeline parallelism is used together with tensor
  parallelism and FlashInfer all-reduce fusion.

  When all-reduce fusion is enabled, the post-expert all-reduce can be deferred and fused into the next layer's
  prepare_attn path. This deferred reduction is tracked by _sglang_needs_allreduce_fusion, and the marker is normally
  consumed by the next layer.

  However, at a PP boundary, hidden states are wrapped into PProxyTensors and sent to the next pipeline stage. The
  Python-side fusion marker is not preserved across this boundary. As a result, one pending post-expert all-reduce
  can be skipped entirely, which leads to larger KL drift in PP+TP runs.

## Modification
  This patch simply flushes the pending MoE all-reduce before returning PPProxyTensors at the PP boundary, so the
  deferred reduction is completed before the tensor is handed to the next PP stage.

  The same issue can also happen with EP, because EP also requires a post-expert all-reduce. This patch handles that
  case as well by flushing the pending EP all-reduce at the PP boundary.
