## Motivation

@humansand

- Add online ModelOpt FP4 quantization for eligible MoE expert weights from BF16, FP16, or FP8 checkpoints.
- Current contract:
  - `modelopt_fp4`:
    - Serialized NVFP4: load packed weights and checkpoint per-tensor FP32 activation scales; missing scales default to `1.0`, and loaded scales overwrite the fallback.
    - BF16/FP16 source: quantize eligible MoE expert weights to NVFP4 at load time; dense and excluded layers keep their source precision.
    - FP8 source: dequantize eligible MoE expert weights to BF16, then quantize them to NVFP4; other layers keep their source quantization.
    - Online conversion uses per-tensor FP32 activation scales. Existing serialized-checkpoint behavior under `SGLANG_FLASHINFER_NVFP4_PER_TOKEN_ACTIVATION=1` is unchanged.
  - `nvfp4_online`:
    - Quantize eligible BF16/FP16/FP8 MoE expert weights at load time with online per-token FP32 activation scales.
    - Support only `flashinfer_trtllm` and `flashinfer_trtllm_routed`; per-tensor activation behavior belongs to `modelopt_fp4`.
  - Draft models:
    - For non-serialized sources, `--quantization modelopt_fp4` is inherited by the draft unless overridden; both target and draft use the generic online MoE conversion path.
    - For a serialized target checkpoint whose BF16 MTP experts are excluded, explicit `--speculative-draft-model-quantization modelopt_fp4` opts the draft into online conversion; inherited draft quantization remains checkpoint-driven.
    - Qwen3.5 MTP distinguishes serialized and online `modelopt_fp4`. Existing DeepSeek, GLM, Bailing, and Kimi NextN/EAGLE draft constructors still opt out and remain outside this generic draft path.
  - Scope: MoE expert weights only. Dense, shared, and excluded layers keep their source representation. MXFP8 source requantization is not supported.
- Related work:
  - Initial online NVFP4 implementation:
    - https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/26083
  - Corrects the activation contract introduced in:
    - https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/31382
    - ~~CuTe DSL with FlashInfer A2A or DeepEP used per-tensor activation scaling under `nvfp4_online`.~~ That behavior is now routed through `modelopt_fp4`.
  - Pending CuTe DSL integration for the `nvfp4_online` per-token activation contract:
    - https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/pull/28354

## Modifications

- Reuse the `nvfp4_online` weight loader through a thin `modelopt_fp4` adapter instead of duplicating conversion logic.
- Use `DefaultModelLoader` for ordinary online `modelopt_fp4`; preserve explicit ModelOpt restore, save, and export workflows.
- Convert FP8 MoE weights through BF16 before NVFP4 quantization while preserving source quantization for other layers.
- Initialize missing per-tensor FP32 activation scales to `1.0`; checkpoint tensors overwrite the fallback.
- Scope exact FP4 quantization math to load-time conversion.
- Track explicit draft quantization separately from target-inherited defaults and route excluded Qwen3.5 MTP experts accordingly.
- Remove stale `nvfp4_online` backend claims inherited from the per-tensor design and document the two activation contracts.
