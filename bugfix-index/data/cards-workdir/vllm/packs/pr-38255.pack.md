## 正文(PR 描述)

## Summary
- Remove spurious "Input tensor shape suggests potential format mismatch" warnings from FLA ops (`chunk_gated_delta_rule` and `chunk_local_cumsum`)
- These warnings fire when `seq_len < num_heads`, which is a normal condition during vLLM inference with chunked prefill or prefix caching (e.g., a 16-token chunk with 32 heads)
- The tensors are always in the correct `[B, T, H, ...]` format -- the heuristic simply does not account for short sequences

Fixes #37103
