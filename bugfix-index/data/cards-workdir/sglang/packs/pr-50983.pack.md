## 正文(PR 描述)

The scheduler's GC freezer reports frozen cyclic garbage as a warning, not as a leak. Some of it comes from code we cannot change: a CI run with the strict check failed on `importlib.metadata` caches, whose `FreezableDefaultDict` holds a lambda that refers back to it.

- `GCFreezer` logs the frozen garbage `refreeze_gc` finds and no longer reports it through the invariant checker's `FailureReporter`, so `SGLANG_ENABLE_STRICT_MEM_CHECK_DURING_IDLE` no longer applies to it.
- `SGLANG_ENABLE_GC_FROZEN_GARBAGE_CHECK` is removed: its only remaining effect was to check on every idle freeze instead of at most every 10 minutes.
