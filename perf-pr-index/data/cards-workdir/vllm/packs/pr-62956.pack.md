```markdown
## Overview

GLM-5.3-Flash decode TPOT **-15% at concurrency 1 on 2x DGX Spark (SM121, TP=2)** by running the BF16 decode GEMMs on the CuTe DSL skinny GEMM instead of cuBLAS. On `nvidia/GLM-5.3-Flash-NVFP4` the attention projections (KDA and MLA), shared experts and LM head stay BF16 and take roughly 46 of a 68 ms decode step on cuBLAS gemv. `glm5next` has no low-latency GEMM module yet, so this adds one modelled on `qwen4_exp/nvidia/low_latency_gemm.py` (#59753, #59632, #61437) with measured SM121 skinny-GEMM plans for the TP=2 local shapes, installed from `Glm5NextForCausalLM` and `Glm5NextMTP`. Supersedes #62949.

## Claims

- 7 local `(N, K)` shapes at M = 1/2/4 (20 entries), routed only on capability `(12, 1)`; other GPUs, dtypes, token counts and shapes keep the standard linear path (cuBLAS).
- Kernel: skinny GEMM is 1.35x to 1.73x faster than cuBLAS gemv at M=1 (238-283 GB/s vs 162-176 GB/s), 1.04x to 1.36x at M=2/4.
- End to end, no speculation, concurrency 1: TPOT 64.0 -> 54.1 ms (-15%), 15.3 -> 18.0 tok/s. With MTP k=3 the decode step is one verify pass at M=4 plus three draft passes at M=1: ITL -3.6%, TPOT -2.7%, 26.7 -> 27.8 tok/s.
- GSM8K (n=100): 93 with cuBLAS, 94 with skinny GEMM.

With MTP the ITL is the decode step (one verify pass at M=4 plus three draft passes at M=1); TPOT and tok/s divide it by the tokens accepted per step. Under greedy decoding the acceptance length stays within 2.85-2.90 across the four runs, so the step gain carries over: TPOT -2.7%, tok/s +3.9%. An earlier 4-request run at model-default sampling gave -12% without speculation (the number in the commit message); with MTP its acceptance length swung 3% between repeats, which hid the step gain.

An nsys census of the skinny GEMM arm shows every listed shape on `CuteSkinnyGemm` in the server (45% of GPU time without speculation, 39.5% with MTP); what is left on cuBLAS is KDA `f_b`/`g_b` (4096, 128) (#57617), the fp32-output router gate (288, 4096) and MLA o_proj at M=1.

Tests: `pytest tests/kernels/test_bf16_skinny_gemm.py -k glm5next`, 26 passed on GB10 (table validity, capability routing, hook installation, 20 selected shapes with cosine > 0.999 against `F.linear`). The selected-shape test skips off capability (12, 1), which CI does not have.

## Details

- Plans come from a CUDA-graph sweep over block_size, outputs_per_block, k_unroll, vector_width and static_k with the weight rotated out of L2, keeping entries that beat cuBLAS by more than 5%. `(2048, 4096)` serves both the shared-expert gate/up and the MLA fused q_a/kv_a projection.
- The replacement method follows the quant method the layer carries: a quant config that excludes `lm_head` gives `ParallelLMHead` an `UnquantizedLinearMethod` rather than an `UnquantizedEmbeddingMethod`, and the head stayed on cuBLAS until this was handled.
- Not covered: TP=1 (the model does not fit one Spark), KDA `f_b`/`g_b` `(4096, 128)` (#57617), MTP `eh_proj` (a plain `nn.Linear`), and M=3, which uses cuBLAS.
- AI assistance (Claude Code) was used for the sweep scripts, the module and the tests; all code and measurements were reviewed and run by the author.
