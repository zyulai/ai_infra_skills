## Purpose
Fix grammatically incorrect error messages in RuntimeError raises.

- `vllm/v1/worker/gpu_worker.py` line 289: `"Not support device type"` → `"Unsupported device type"`

"Not support" is grammatically incorrect English. "Unsupported" is the correct adjective form.
