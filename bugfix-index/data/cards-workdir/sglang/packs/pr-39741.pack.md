## 正文(PR 描述)

Fixes #38854

### Motivation
In Qwen3.5 / Qwen3.6 / Qwen3Next GDN checkpoints such as `XReyRobert/Qwopus3.6-27B-v2-GPTQ-Pro-v1`, `in_proj_b` and `in_proj_a` (total fused width $N = 96$ at `num_v_heads = 48`) are left as unquantized BF16 `.weight` tensors in safetensors, while `config.json` `quantization_config.dynamic` omits `in_proj_a`/`in_proj_b`. Previously SGLang built `in_proj_ba` as GPTQ/Marlin anyway, dropping the BF16 `.weight` tensors and aborting in `gptq_marlin_repack` (`size_n = 96 is not divisible by tile_n_size = 64`).

Conversely, inferring dense storage purely from layer shape (`size_n % 64 != 0`) or partial fused-shard exclusions would silently allocate uninitialized BF16 weights whenever quantized shards (`.qweight`/`.scales`) exist (e.g., 64-head `in_proj_ba` at TP=4 or partial dynamic exclusions like `-:.*in_proj_z$`).

### Modifications
1. **Checkpoint-Grounded Dense vs. Quantized Init (No Shape Fallback)**:
   - In `python/sglang/srt/model_loader/weight_utils.py`, added `inspect_safetensors_tensor_names` and attached checkpoint tensor names to GPTQ / `gptq`-backed `MoeWNA16Config` configs during `get_quant_config` (supporting local index/shards, cached HF artifacts, and Hub index/header inspection while respecting `HF_HUB_OFFLINE` and `LoadFormat.DUMMY`).
   - In `python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/gptq/gptq.py`, added `_CheckpointTensorManifest` (O(1) `__deepcopy__`, `apply_weight_name_mapper`, canonical wrapper prefix normalization), `_GPTQCheckpointMixin`, and `_get_scheme_linear_quant_method` across `GPTQConfig`, `GPTQMarlinConfig`, `CPUGPTQConfig`, and `GPTQAscendConfig` (keeping `LinearBase` / `ParallelLMHead` guards and per-layer dynamic schemes intact). Dense `UnquantizedLinearMethod` is chosen strictly from `quantization_config.dynamic` exclusions or checkpoint metadata showing `.weight` without `.qweight`, rejecting mixed dense/quantized shards.
   - In `python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/moe_wna16.py`, added cached `_gptq_config` delegation for `linear_quant_method == "gptq"` (`set_checkpoint_tensor_names`, `apply_weight_name_mapper`, `update_packed_modules_mapping`, `is_linear_unquantized`, and `uses_checkpoint_tensor_manifest` gated off for AWQ) so `--quantization moe_wna16` also handles dense GDN projections and dynamic exclusions.
   - In `python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/marlin_utils.py` and `gptq.py`, added `verify_marlin_supports_layer` so quantized layers with Marlin-incompatible partition shapes fail loudly instead of silently falling back to BF16.
   - Removed the `ba_out_per_part % 64 != 0` heuristic from `python/sglang/srt/models/qwen3_5.py` and `python/sglang/srt/models/qwen3_next.py`.
2. **Normalized Fused-Shard `get_dynamic_override`, 3-bit `Fraction`, & Load-Time Precision Checks**:
   - Updated `get_dynamic_override` (`python/sglang/srt/layers/quantization/utils.py`) with `resolve_dynamic_fused_shards` (checking `packed_modules_mapping` and `_DYNAMIC_FUSED_SHARDS`) to inspect all shards of fused projections (`gate_up_proj`, `qkv_proj`, `in_proj_qkvz`, `in_proj_ba`), returning `False` only when the fused name or all shards are excluded, raising `ValueError` on partial exclusions or conflicting effective settings, and normalizing against global config defaults so redundant explicit overrides (e.g., `bits=4`) succeed without fabricating un-overridden keys.
   - Preserved `Fraction(32, config.weight_bits)` in `override_config` for 3-bit GPTQ (`pack_factor = 32/3`).
   - Added `validate_quant_param_compatibility` in `utils.py` and wired it into `load_weights` across `qwen3_5.py` and `qwen3_next.py` to reject checkpoint/parameter precision mismatches loudly while remaining PP-safe.
3. **GDN & CPU Packed Weight Loader Fixes**:
   - Updated `Qwen3_5GatedDeltaNet._bind_packed_weight_loaders` (`qwen3_5.py`) and `Qwen3GatedDeltaNet._bind_packed_weight_loaders` (`qwen3_next.py`) to bind GPTQ params (`qweight`, `qzeros`, `scales`, `g_idx`) instead of assuming `.weight`, round `Fraction` split sizes for 3-bit GPTQ, and pass non-output-sharded parameters (`output_dim is None`, e.g., GPTQ `g_idx`) cleanly through `_make_packed_weight_loader`.
   - Fixed `MergedColumnParallelLinear._load_fused_module_from_checkpoint` (`python/sglang/srt/layers/linear.py`) under `_is_cpu` to adjust `pad_output_sizes` with `adjust_shard_indexes_for_packing` when `packed_dim == output_dim`, preventing 8× zero-padding corruption on CPU `qzeros`.
4. **Unit Tests**:
   - Added `test/registered/unit/models/test_qwen3_5_gdn_gptq_unquant_fallback.py` covering all-shard vs. partial/mixed/default-normalized `get_dynamic_override`, 3-bit `Fraction` overrides, checkpoint manifest resolution, `CPUGPTQConfig`/`GPTQAscendConfig`/`MoeWNA16Config` dense & override resolution, strict Marlin partition verification, `Qwen3_5`/`Qwen3Next` init and GPTQ `in_proj_qkv` split loading (GPU & CPU), `qzeros` CPU packed padding, and `load_weights` precision-mismatch rejection.
