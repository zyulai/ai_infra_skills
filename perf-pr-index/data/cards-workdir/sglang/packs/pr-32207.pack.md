## Motivation

PP and MTP are currently incompatible. However, we frequently need to enable MTP on decode nodes. If prefill nodes enable PP without turning on MTP, decode nodes will be missing part of the draft_kv. Our empirical tests show that the acceptance rate of MTP on the decode side declines as a result. Therefore, we implemented adaptations to enable compatibility between PP and MTP on prefill nodes
