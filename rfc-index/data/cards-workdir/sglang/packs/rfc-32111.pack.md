## 正文(PRD / 提案全文)

To reduce the complexity of the MLA attention backend paths, we plan to deprecate `--attention-backend cutlass_mla`.

| Quantization/Backend | What it is | Why it's removable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cutlass MLA** | Decode-only MLA kernel, gated to exactly SM 10.0 (B200); already disabled on SM 10.3 (GB300) | `CutlassMLABackend` only overrides `forward_decode` — prefill, extend, verify, and draft-extend all fall through to its own parent class, `FlashInferMLABackend`. It's also locked to `page_size=128` and blocks tree speculative decode. It hasn't had a functional change in ~10 months (the only recent touch was disabling it on newer Blackwell hardware), and it isn't exercised by any CI workflow. |

We recommend `trtllm_mla` or `tokenspeed_mla` as the supported replacements for MLA decode. Their perf. is much better.

Feedback time: 3 weeks.
