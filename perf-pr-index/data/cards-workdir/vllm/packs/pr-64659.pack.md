## Overview

This PR adds sequence parallelism (SP) to MiniMax-M3 on NVIDIA when running TP + EP with the MegaMoE backend, plus the GEMM-RS kernel for `o_proj` and the dense MLP `down_proj`. The design follows the existing Kimi-K3 and DeepSeek-V4.1 SP.

Without SP, MegaMoE already shards tokens across TP ranks internally, but then writes its shard into a zero-filled full-size buffer, and the next RMSNorm all-reduces it. So every layer pays 2 full all-reduces: one after attention, and one on a mostly-zero MoE output. With SP, the residual stream stays sharded by token, and each MoE layer does 1 all-gather before attention and 1 reduce-scatter after `o_proj`. MegaMoE consumes the shard directly. The 3 dense layers do one more all-gather/reduce-scatter pair around their MLP.

## Claims

- SP is enabled automatically for TP > 1 + EP + MegaMoE + PP1, same rule as DeepSeek-V4.1 and Kimi-K3. GEMM-RS is opt-in with `VLLM_ENABLE_GEMM_RS=1`.
- On 4×GB300 with `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3.1-Flash` (8k in / 1k out, DSpark spec decode), compared to TP4 + EP without SP:
  - P50 TTFT is 14-20% lower at all concurrencies.
  - Output throughput is +6.5% at concurrency 16 and +13.6% at concurrency 64, a tie at concurrency 4, and -4% at concurrency 1 (see below).
- gsm8k is unchanged.

## Details

Changes in `vllm/models/minimax_m3/nvidia/model.py`:
- **Model forward**: shard the hidden states after the embedding, and shard the MegaMoE padding mask once. At the end, all-gather the output together with the EAGLE3/DSpark aux hidden states in 1 collective.
- **Attention**: all-gather the input, reduce-scatter the output (`o_proj` + `sp_reduce_scatter`, or GEMM-RS).
- **MegaMoE**: takes the shard as-is (`input_is_sequence_sharded`), so no shard/gather/zero-fill. The existing `use_sequence_parallel` flag in `MiniMaxM3MoE` is renamed to `shard_replicated_input`, since it means the opposite of model-level SP.
- **Dense MLP** (3 of 60 layers): all-gather, `gate_up` (keeps the fused dense GEMM1) and `down_proj`, then reduce-scatter.
- **Fusions**: the AR + Gemma RMSNorm fusions are skipped under SP, since there is nothing left to all-reduce.

**GEMM-RS with a quantized input** Above its token threshold, the fused dense GEMM1 outputs an MXFP8 `QuantizedActivation` instead of BF16. So `GemmRsAr.apply()` now accepts it and skips its own quantization. The scales are already in the F8_128x4 swizzled layout that the kernel expects.

**Limitations and follow-ups**
- MTP blocks and PP > 1 don't use SP.
- The dense MLP stays TP-sharded instead of adding Kimi-K3's replicated option, since it is only 3 layers.
- The all-gathers move BF16. Gathering the MXFP8 `qkv_proj` input instead would halve the bytes; this is left for a follow-up.
