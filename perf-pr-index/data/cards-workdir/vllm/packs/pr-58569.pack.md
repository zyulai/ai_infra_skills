## Purpose

Avoid an extra copy by storing the sparse prefill output in place. 

This is a **tiny** perf improvement (5us per MLA layer), meaning a total of ~55us per forward. With ~13ms per forward this means it's not quite measurable within the noise from vllm bench serve. Hence will skip full perf sweep and only provide correctness results and simple non-regression benchmark.

## Why 

- The attention output has shape `[s, h, kv_lora_rank]`, i.e. without the rope dim
- The kernel's output is sized like the query, i.e. `[s, h, kv_lora_rank + rope_dim]`
- Once the kernel completes, it slices out the first `:kv_lora_rank` part of the head dimension.

We delegate this last part to triton instead. We do this simply by masking the store so that any workgroup responsible for a block in the head dimension >=`kv_lora_rank` is diverged.
