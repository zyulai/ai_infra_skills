## Motivation

On CUDA the JIT rmsnorm kernels were only used on the HF-semantics path; `RMSNorm.forward_cuda` ran flashinfer for the plain, fused-add and per-tensor FP8 variants. The flashinfer CuTe-DSL kernels pay two CTA barriers, three shared-memory passes and a weight re-read per row, and issue nothing before `griddepcontrol.wait`; at decode sizes they leave 0.3-0.8 us per launch on the table on B200, and at hidden sizes below 4096 they stream at 3.8-4.6 TB/s.

## Modifications

- `elementwise/rmsnorm.cuh`: one template kernel with an `RMSNormMode` (`PLAIN`, `FUSED_ADD`, `QUANT`, `FUSED_ADD_QUANT`) and an HF-rounding flag. One row per `threads_per_row` threads, `rows_per_cta` rows per CTA, 32 B vectors on Blackwell (16 B elsewhere); the row stays in registers, the weight slice is loaded before `griddepcontrol.wait`, rows spanning several warps reduce with one barrier, short rows (32-496) take a power-of-two slice of a warp. Sum of squares and epilogue use `fma.rn.f32.bf16` on Blackwell (`out = T(fma(x, w, -0) * r)`, one rounding, no converts); the fused residual sum is one packed `add.rn.bf16x2` and the normalized value is the rounded residual; fp8 outputs are `cvt.rn.satfinite.e4m3x2.f32` pairs packed into 16 B stores. Replaces `fused_add_rmsnorm.cuh` and `rmsnorm_hf.cuh`.
- `ops/layernorm/norm.py`: the schedule `(vec_bytes, threads_per_row, rows_per_cta)` is chosen in Python from measurements (4-8 warps per row, 2 vectors per thread for the plain op and 1 at decode for the fused op, packed rows for short rows at large token counts), both schedules of a signature are built at once with `parallel_compile`, and the token count picks one at call time. Public entry points `rmsnorm`, `fused_add_rmsnorm`, `rmsnorm_quant`, `fused_add_rmsnorm_quant`; `cast_x_before_out_mul=True` replaces `rmsnorm_hf`. Supported hidden sizes: multiples of 16 in [32, 16384].
- `srt/layers/layernorm.py`: `RMSNorm.forward_cuda` and `forward_with_per_tensor_quant_fusion` use the JIT family on SM100 for bf16 / fp16 (static gate per layer); other archs keep flashinfer. The HF path uses it on every CUDA arch, as before.
- Registry entries, `bench_norm.py` quant benchmarks, `test_rmsnorm.py` rewritten (plain, fused, HF, quant, strided, empty) with a parallel prebuild fixture; `test_fused_add_rmsnorm.py` and `test_rmsnorm_hf.py` folded in.

## Accuracy

Plain output within 1 bf16 ulp of flashinfer on < 2e-4 of the elements (accumulation order); fused residual bit-exact to `(x.float() + res.float()).to(bf16)`; HF output bit-exact to the fp32 reference; fp8 bits equal to flashinfer except <= 2e-6 of the elements for `rmsnorm_quant`. `test_rmsnorm.py`: 2777 passed (full sweep) on B200.
