## PR body
```markdown
Follow-up to #46917 and #46925.

- `auto` now uses the Engram `all_to_all` lookup for decode batches and inside CUDA graphs too, not only for eager prefill. Only a prefill batch of uneven per-rank counts captured in a CUDA graph keeps the earlier collective.
- Equal per-rank counts (MAX_LEN batches, CUDA graphs) take the fixed-size collectives, which a graph can capture: `all_gatherv` already reduces to an all-gather for them, and the exchange uses `reduce_scatter_tensor` or `all_to_all_single`; uneven counts keep the variable-size ones.
- `auto` is resolved in one place, `resolve_engram_config` in `engram.py`: once per process at model construction, when the parallel layout is final, and recorded in `engram_config` (log: `Engram lookup auto -> ...`). The layers run the resolved lookup, and the per-step `_dp_exchange` choice is gone; server-argument resolution only validates explicit values.
- The DP lookup is one method per exchange: `_dp_reduce_scatter_lookup` and `_dp_all_to_all_lookup` beside `_dp_global_buffer_lookup`.
- An explicit `reduce_scatter` or `all_to_all` now applies to decode as well; `all_reduce` keeps the collectives used before `auto`.
- Docs: `auto` and its decode measurements.

Behavior change: DP-attention decode on CUDA with columns that divide over the TP group moves from the reduce-scatter to the all-to-all; the output is bitwise the same.

## Measurements

GB300, one host-table Engram layer, rank 1, us per layer:

| DP | Tokens per rank | `all_reduce` (us) | `reduce_scatter` (us) | `all_to_all` (us) | `auto` (us) |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | prefill 32K | 3,479.0 | 2,645.6 | 2,478.2 | 2,475.1 |
| 2 | decode 16 | 31.4 | 28.2 | 37.3 | 35.2 |
| 2 | decode 400 | 72.3 | 68.5 | 65.3 | 65.2 |
| 2 | decode 1,280 | 135.0 | 130.7 | 123.7 | 124.3 |
| 4 | prefill 32K | 5,861.4 | 3,819.1 | 2,463.2 | 2,460.4 |
| 4 | decode 16 | 49.6 | 33.7 | 42.1 | 41.6 |
| 4 | decode 400 | 105.5 | 101.2 | 69.2 | 70.1 |
| 4 | decode 1,280 | 193.3 | 187.1 | 130.5 | 131.2 |

Before this PR, decode used the reduce-scatter. In a decode microbenchmark under CUDA graphs on GB300, B300 and H200 (DP 2-8, 16 to 1,280 tokens per rank), `all_to_all` was faster in 35 of 48 shapes: from DP 4 it was up to 48% faster and at most 12 us behind at 64 tokens per rank or fewer; at DP 2 it was at most 20 us behind and up to 53 us ahead.

## Verification

- Bitwise check of the `EngramEmbedding` DP lookup on GB300 at DP 2 and 4, every `lookup` choice against `all_reduce`: prefill (uniform, skewed, one empty rank) and decode captured in a CUDA graph and replayed with new ids. All equal.
- `test_deepseek_v41_flash_gb300_dp2ep2.py` on GB300: passed, GSM8K 0.98, MMMU-Pro 0.79.
- The same recipe at attention DP 4 / EP 4, GSM8K: 0.985.
```
