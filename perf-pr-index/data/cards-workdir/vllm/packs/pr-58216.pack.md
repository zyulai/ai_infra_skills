## PR body
```markdown
Adds a new `diffusion_constrained` property that allows us to reduce the amount of work needed when performing constrained diffusion (ie: structured answers).

Experimental results: approx. 25% faster.

Followup to #57250 

## Purpose

By passing `logprob_token_ids` it means that you already know the possible answer set. `diffusion_constrained: true` allows us to save work - the unembedding is a GEMM over the K gathered rows of the tied embedding instead of all 262k, the sampler runs in K-space (Gumbel sample, argmax, entropy, convergence), and maps picks back through the set, and the self-conditioning soft embed multiplies K-space probs by the K embedding rows. Overall we save ~25% in my local experiments on a GB10.


<p>The timings, warm engine, fresh prompts per arm, two runs of each arm shown as a range. Single-slot reads, one step, canvas 48:</p>

read | arm | concurrency 1 latency, median | concurrency 1 answers/s | concurrency 32 latency, median / p90 | concurrency 32 answers/s
-- | -- | -- | -- | -- | --
yes/no | plain | 105 to 106 ms | 9.5 | 551 to 561 / 575 to 621 ms | 56.7 to 56.9
yes/no | constrained | 95 to 96 ms | 10.5 | 420 / 430 to 440 ms | 76.0 to 76.2
choice (4-way) | plain | 98 to 100 ms | 10.0 to 10.2 | 502 to 530 / 590 ms | 60.0 to 60.1
choice (4-way) | constrained | 88 to 92 ms | 10.9 to 11.4 | 354 to 382 / 445 to 457 ms | 81.1 to 82.8


At concurrency 1 the constraint takes about 10 percent off. At concurrency 32 it is worth 34 to 38 percent more answers per second and about a quarter less latency, because the batched step spends a much larger share of its time in the vocabulary-sized work.
```
