## 正文(PR 描述)

DeepSeek updated the chat template yesterday: [link](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1/commit/8a58a132790c9935686eb97f042afa8013451c9f).

They added the `<think>` token to the chat template, indicating that the first section represents the thinking process and won't output `<think>` in the final output. This PR aims to address the reasoning parser implementation.

We maintain compatibility to support both styles:

- `<think>...</think>...` in streaming and full generation.
- `...</think>...` in streaming and full generation.

FIX https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/12999
