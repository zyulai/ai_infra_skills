## 正文(PRD / 提案全文)

### Motivation.

Description:
This proposal introduces an enhancement to the rejection sampling mechanism at the core of speculative decoding. The current method relies on a fixed, context-independent random threshold, which leads to a significant "random rejection" problem. In high-uncertainty generation scenarios, plausible candidate tokens proposed by the draft model are frequently rejected merely because their acceptance ratio falls slightly below this fixed threshold due to random chance. This wastes the computational effort spent on verifying subsequent draft tokens and severely limits the acceleration potential of speculative decoding for creative or open-ended tasks.

Motivation:
We observe a dramatic drop in draft acceptance rates as generation uncertainty increases—from over 80% at temperature=0 to only around 30% at temperature=1. This efficiency loss stems from the fixed threshold's inability to account for the intrinsic uncertainty of the target model's predictions at different generation steps. There is a clear need for an adaptive threshold mechanism that is aware of the model's confidence. Such a mechanism could intelligently relax acceptance criteria in high-uncertainty scenarios, reducing wasteful rejections and substantially improving inference throughput without significantly compromising output quality.



### Proposed Change.

Proposed Solution:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13194 propose the Efficient Adaptive Rejection Sampling (EARS) method. Its core innovation is to dynamically adjust the acceptance threshold based on the target model's predictive confidence:
1、Define Uncertainty: Measure the model's uncertainty at the current step as Uncertainty = 1 - max(P_target).
2、Compute Dynamic Tolerance: Introduce a tolerance term proportional to this uncertainty: Tolerance = β * (1 - max(P_target)), where β is a tunable hyperparameter.
3、Modify Acceptance Condition: Change the traditional rule P_target / P_draft ≥ U (where U is a uniform random number) to:
P_target / P_draft ≥ U - Tolerance.

When the model is highly uncertain (low max(P_target), high Tolerance), the effective acceptance threshold is lowered, "pardoning" plausible candidates that would have been randomly rejected. When the model is confident (high max(P_target), low Tolerance), the criterion remains strict, preserving generation quality.

Key Benefits:
1、Significant Efficiency Gains: Achieves up to ~18.12% higher token throughput in open-ended generation tasks.
2、Minimal Impact on Quality: Incurs only a negligible accuractony drop (e.g., <=0.84% on GSM8K) in tasks requiring precision.
3、Easy Integration: Requires no modifications to model architectures. It can be seamlessly integrated into existing speculative decoding frameworks with minimal engineering overhead via optimizations like pre-computation.
