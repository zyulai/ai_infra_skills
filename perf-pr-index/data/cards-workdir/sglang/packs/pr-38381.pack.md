## PR body

Reverts sgl-project/sglang#37376

## Why

#37376 enables a fused silu+mul+FP8-quant kernel on the block-wise FP8 MoE path. It is not numerically equivalent to the two-kernel path it replaces, and the difference is large enough to cost speculative-decoding acceptance on GLM-5.2-FP8 EAGLE MTP.

`test/registered/e2e/models/test_dsa_glm52_tp_mtp.py::test_bs_1_speed` asserts `accept_length > 4.0` and is currently failing on main.

## Bisect

Two `Rerun Test` dispatches of that test on the `8-gpu-h200` pool, at #37376 and its direct parent:

| commit | Acc Length | Tokens | Speed (token/s) | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `503511c963` (parent) | **4.727** | 1092 | 323.28 | pass |
| `a8b2f36dee` (#37376) | **3.949** | 778 | 265.40 | fail |

- [run at parent](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/34173127970) / [run at #37376](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/actions/runs/34173132321)
- 4.727 is the value every scheduled main run produced before #37376 landed; 3.949 reproduces identically across all three attempts in the run.
- Net effect on this recipe: **-16.5% accept length, -18% bs=1 decode speed**.

## Mechanism

- `_can_use_fused_silu_mul_quant_fp8` turns the kernel on from shape/dtype alone. There is no flag or env var to disable it, so an affected model cannot opt out.
- The fused kernel emits the FP8 down-GEMM input directly, skipping the bf16 `intermediate_cache2` round-trip, so its output differs from the two-kernel path.
- The unit test added in #37376 states the tolerance explicitly: `fp8_match > 0.95` (up to 5% of FP8 bytes may differ) and `assert_close(fused_output, fallback_output, rtol=0.02, atol=0.02)`.
- Accuracy-style evals absorb that (`cosine_sim > 0.9999`), but acceptance length is a discrete draft/target token-agreement rate compounded over 5 draft steps, and target and draft share the same MoE, so the perturbation accumulates instead of averaging out.

## Note

Reverting rather than gating because no switch exists today. A reland could keep the kernel behind an opt-in flag, or disable it when speculative decoding is enabled.
