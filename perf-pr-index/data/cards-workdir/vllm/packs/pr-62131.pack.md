## Overview

DSv4.1-Flash mono decode (MI355X, `--monokernel`): the DSpark draft's three layers now run on the mono K1 / K2 kernels too, as ATOM's `DraftMonoRunner` does, instead of vLLM's per-op path. Follow-up to #60397 and #61082; they follow `--monokernel` like the target's layers, with no new switch. Overlaps files with #60731 (gfx942 port), so whichever merges second needs a rebase.

## Claims

- The draft's three layers cost about 227 us of kernel time per step on mono, in place of about 640 us on the original path (graph profile, TP4 c1): about 414 us less GPU time a step.
- AgentX at c1, per-request ITL p50 is 0.030 ms (1.8%) lower than InferenceX's run with `--monokernel`, at TP4 (1.646 against 1.678 ms) and at TP2 (1.970 against 2.000 ms).
- Draft quality is unchanged: DSpark mean acceptance length 3.889 / 3.935 (TP4) and 3.917 (TP2) against 3.899 / 3.896 without, and GSM8K 0.975 / 0.972.
- Target layers' kernels are untouched: their ISA is identical to `main`'s.

## Details

The draft layers are `DeepseekV4DecoderLayer`s with window-only attention (compress ratio 0, no indexer), so the kernels need no new stage. What differs is carried by the build and the step's integers:

- **Routing:** 128 routed experts, top-3 (`dspark_n_routed_experts` / `dspark_num_experts_per_tok`) and one FP4 group per ug task. `MoeBuild` already took these; `MonoBuild` now carries `experts`, `topk`, `ug_groups` and `swa_width` into the MoE stage and the scratch layout.
- **SWA rows:** the draft's metadata is non-causal, with rows `get_dspark_swa_index_width(window, num_speculative_tokens)` wide (192 for window 128 and 5 drafts). K1's key-table stage read the row width as the constant 128; it reads it from the build now.
- **First layer:** the draft hands it the embedding already repeated to the hc copies, `[rows, hc, dim]`; K1 takes it as the settled residual (`fold=False`), as for the target's layer 0.
- **Its own runner:** the scratch layout depends on the number of experts, and a mailbox word must never be a tag to one launch and data to another, so the draft's layers share a second `DSV41MonoLayer` (epoch, scratch, peer buffers) as in ATOM. Every rank creates it at the same layer of the same eager step, like the target's.

Target builds are unchanged: every new field defaults to the target's value, the kernel symbols gain no suffix for them, and the ISA diff above is empty.

Limitations: MI355X (CDNA4), TP2 / TP4, steps of at most 48 rows (the draft's are 5 per request, so up to 8 requests); the layer comparison is on eager steps only. The draft's remaining per-step cost is outside its layers: `_prepare_dflash_inputs` (3 launches of 113 us), the six skinny head GEMMs (279 us), and about 75 small elementwise and copy kernels. I found no open PR doing this. Developed and measured with Claude Code; the submitter's line-by-line review is still pending.
