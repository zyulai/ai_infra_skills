## Overview

On CDNA3 (gfx942) and CDNA4 (gfx950), the prefill launch profile of `qsa_sparse_paged_attention` (more than 512 programs) now uses one warp per program, except BF16 K/V with `BLOCK_M >= 16` on gfx942, which uses `BLOCK_N=32`. This makes Qwen3.8-Flash-Next's prefill sparse attention 1.2-2.2x faster, and long-prompt serving 2-3% faster at TP2, with unchanged accuracy.

Co-authored with @eppaneamd: the gfx950 one-warp rule comes from his #65341, which was closed and folded into this PR. Not a duplicate: draft #65474 is stacked on this PR and retunes the same branch further, and #57969 changes `BLOCK_M` on gfx11 only.

## Claims

- Kernel: 1.6-2.2x at TP1/TP2 and 1.2-1.8x at TP4/TP8 at 8,192 rows, on MI325X and MI355X, for BF16 and FP8 K/V. No measured cell is slower than main.
- Outputs are bitwise equal to main, except gfx942 BF16 at TP1/TP2 (`BLOCK_N=32`). There, outputs differ by at most 2.0e-3 absolute, with the same error against an fp64 reference as main.
- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8 TP2, long-prompt cells (32k and 128k prefill, 60k/600): throughput +1.5 to +3.4% on MI325X with BF16 KV cache, +2.0 to +3.4% with FP8 KV cache, and +1.6 to +3.1% on MI355X. Decode-heavy cells (1k/1k, 8k/1k) are within ±1%, except +2.4-2.5% on MI325X at 8k/1k with concurrency 32 and 128. Median ITL is unchanged.
- No change in GSM8K (10 fixed, 8 broken, McNemar p = 0.82) or in RULER NIAH (single_2) at 64k and 128k (1.000 on both arms).

## Details

The >512-program branch was tuned on GB300 (`BLOCK_N=64`, 1 split, 2 warps). On ROCm it now selects:

| Arch | K/V | `BLOCK_M` | `(BLOCK_N, warps)` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gfx942 | BF16 | >= 16 (TP1, TP2) | (32, 2) |
| gfx942 | FP8, or BF16 with `BLOCK_M` < 16 | any | (64, 1) |
| gfx950 | BF16, FP8 | any | (64, 1) |
| other ROCm, CUDA | any | any | (64, 2), unchanged |

- **Why two rules on gfx942.** One warp is faster on both archs everywhere except gfx942 BF16 at `BLOCK_M >= 16`, where `BLOCK_N=32` is faster (2.2x vs about 1.5x at TP2). The two can't be combined: `BLOCK_N=32` with one warp is `HSA_STATUS_ERROR_MEMORY_FAULT` on gfx942 with BF16 at `BLOCK_M=16`. Hence the `elif`.
- **Why FP8 is excluded from `BLOCK_N=32`.** The kernel takes FP8 K/V. With FP8, `BLOCK_N=32` gains only about 1.2x and regresses TP2 at 520 rows (0.78x), while one warp gives 1.8-1.9x.
- **Gate.** `get_cdna_version()` returns 3 only on gfx942 and 4 only on gfx950. Other ROCm archs, CUDA and the <= 512-program profiles are unchanged.

**Limitations and CI gaps.**

- In `.buildkite/test-amd.yaml` only `:amd: (MI355 DPX) Qwen4 Exp` runs `test_qsa_amd.py`, and that step is `optional: true`. No MI300 step runs it, so the gfx942 branches are covered only by the manual runs above.
- End to end was measured at TP2 only. The kernel table covers the TP1, TP4 and TP8 shapes. MI300X and MI350X were not measured.
- BLOCK_N=16 with one warp is a further 1.2-1.3x at >= 2,048 rows but slower at 520-1,024 rows, so it needs splits chosen from the program count. #65474, stacked on this PR, does that for BF16 K/V on both archs.
- The 24q/1kv shape (`BLOCK_M=32`) is not a Qwen3.8-Flash-Next shape and is not in the tests.
