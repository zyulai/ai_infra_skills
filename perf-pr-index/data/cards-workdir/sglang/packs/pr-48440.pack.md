## Motivation

In Kimi-K3's MoE (E = 896, top-16, block_m 32), small batches go through a patched sort kernel that also quantizes the stage-1 input to MXFP8. Until now it covered at most 256 (token, expert) pairs, i.e. 16 tokens at top-16; larger batches fell back to aiter's opus sort + quant.

On MI355X the patched sort is **31–46% faster** than aiter's sort + quant up to 960 pairs, and only ties from 1152 pairs, where its bitonic sort width doubles past 1024. The 256-pair limit therefore left decode/verify batches of 17–64 tokens on the slower path.

## Modifications

`python/sglang/kernels/ops/moe/moe_sorting_small.py`:

- Raise the limit from 256 to 1024 (token, expert) pairs (64 tokens at top-16).
- Rename `_EMIT_MAX_PAIRS` to `_SMALL_SORT_MAX_PAIRS`, and use this one constant both for picking the patched sort and for its stage-1 quant, which previously had separate hard-coded `256` checks.

Batches above 1024 pairs still use aiter's sort + quant.

## Accuracy Tests

The patched sort's outputs (sorted ids, weights, expert ids, and the quantized stage-1 input) are **byte-identical** to aiter's at 384–1024 pairs, so model outputs do not change.
