## PR body
```markdown
This PR avoids the M dimensions (num_tokens) padding to 4x (that is required by Hopper CUTLASS) in most cases due to padding of cuda graphs and their capture sizes. 

For DSR1-FP8 running on 8xH200 GPUs with batch size 32:

**Before PR:**
Mean TPOT (ms):                          31.41    

**After PR**
Mean TPOT (ms):                          30.40 

=> 3.3% TPOT improvement


```
