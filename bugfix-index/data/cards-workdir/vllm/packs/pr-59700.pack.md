## 正文(PR 描述)

## Summary

`tests/v1/distributed/test_dbo.py::test_dbo_dp_ep_gsm8k[deepep_high_throughput]` has failed on amd-ci nightly with accuracy `0.617` against `MIN_ACCURACY = 0.62` on at least two separate occasions over the past 90 days, on two different ROCm architectures:

- MI300: https://buildkite.com/vllm/amd-ci/builds/13015/summary?jid=01a0a973-18d1-4f3f-925a-d78c307267cb&tab=output
- MI355: https://buildkite.com/vllm/amd-ci/builds/13954/summary?jid=01a0f6b2-7bd8-486c-b337-f85dfee4cf7d&tab=output

Both failed with the identical message:
```
AssertionError: DBO+DP+EP accuracy too low (deepep_high_throughput): 0.617 < 0.620
```

The eval samples at `temperature=0.3` (from the model's `generation_config.json`) over only 256 GSM8K questions with no fixed per-request seed, so sub-1%-point misses are within expected sampling noise at this sample size rather than necessarily indicating a regression.

This PR widens `MIN_ACCURACY` from `0.62` to `0.61` **on ROCm only**, leaving CUDA/other platforms unchanged.
