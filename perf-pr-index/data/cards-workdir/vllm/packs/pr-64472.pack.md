## Overview

Preserve KV cache zeroing when mixed-precision `UniformTypeKVCacheSpecs` groups are reduced for the scheduler. Resolve the zeroing decision across workers so pipeline stages agree even when only one stage has mixed precision. Split out of #63232 (MiniMax-M3.1-Flash); this change remains independent of the other split PRs.

## Claims

- Scheduler projection preserves the decision to zero newly allocated attention blocks.
- All PP workers initialize zeroing when any worker requires it. This prevents MRV2 from receiving zeroing work on a stage without a zeroer.
- Uniform-precision attention-only configurations continue to skip zeroing.

## Details

`generate_scheduler_kv_cache_config` reduces each `UniformTypeKVCacheSpecs` to one layer spec. Recomputing mixed precision after that reduction loses information. Saving only worker 0's mixed-precision result also leaves PP workers inconsistent: a uniform stage skips zeroer initialization although it receives the scheduler's `new_block_ids_to_zero`.

`has_mixed_precision_kv_cache` remains a local fact. `needs_kv_cache_zeroing` becomes a stored execution decision, initialized from each worker's full configuration. The planner ORs that decision across workers after final replanning, and scheduler projection preserves it through deepcopy. The tradeoff is that uniform stages also zero new attention blocks when another stage requires zeroing.

This extends the metadata-preservation fix in #49623 to intra-group precision and PP worker agreement. It is not a duplicate of the open fixes for transfer destinations/tails (#62313, #55085), backend-specific block reuse (#58716), or initial allocation zero fill (#59432).
