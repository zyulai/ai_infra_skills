## 正文(PR 描述)

## Summary
- replace the previously skipped priority preemption test with a deterministic multi-step regression
- simulate scheduler/model iterations until block-boundary KV pressure can trigger preemption
- assert both outcomes: low-priority preemption and eventual scheduling of the high-priority request

## Root cause
- the old test expected immediate preemption after a single schedule step
- in practice, preemption occurs after running requests advance and request additional KV blocks
