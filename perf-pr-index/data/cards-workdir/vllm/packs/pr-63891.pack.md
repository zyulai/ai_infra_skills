## Overview

This PR runs #60731's `fused_markov` DSpark sampler (one launch per draft position) on gfx950 (MI355X) with `--monokernel`, for steps of up to 5 requests. Today it runs only on gfx942, so gfx950 runs vLLM's loop of about 51 kernels per decode step at k=6. On MI355X, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash TP4 c1 decode steps get 2.4% faster at equal GSM8K and AL. It interacts with #63181, #63486 and #61311 (see Details).

## Claims

- With `--monokernel` on gfx950, a DSpark decode step's Markov sampling takes k+1 = 7 launches instead of about 51. Measured with the torch profiler, TP4, 2 rounds:
  - GPU kernels per decode step go from 365-366 to 321 (net -44 to -46);
  - the PR runs 7.00-7.01 `_markov_*` kernels per step.
- On gfx950 the fused launches take steps of at most 5 requests (`MAX_ROWS_GFX950`). From 6 on, vLLM's loop is faster there. Both archs launch the step kernel at 4 warps.
- On MI355X, `main` 335dd77b09 vs this PR (c128b8ba44):
  - Workload: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash `--monokernel`, DSpark k=6, AV off, synthetic AL 3.87, random 32k in / 1k out, `--ignore-eos`. Step = ITL p50.
  - Runs: 4 fresh servers per arm in ABBA order, each with 3 runs of 32 prompts. TP2 runs Engram on the host.

  | Config | `main` | PR | Delta | Spread across servers (`main` / PR) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | TP4 c1 | 5.372 ms | 5.245 ms | **-127 us (-2.36%)** | 0.39% / 0.44% |
  | TP4 c2 | 6.637 ms | 6.498 ms | **-139 us (-2.10%)** | 1.02% / 0.41% |
  | TP4 c4 | 8.614 ms | 8.530 ms | **-84 us (-0.98%)** | 0.91% / 0.49% |

  | TP2 c1 | 6.434 ms | 6.288 ms | **-146 us (-2.27%)** | 0.52% / 1.01% |
- GSM8K-1319 and AL are unchanged (6 passes per arm):

  | Parallel requests | `main` GSM8K / AL | PR GSM8K / AL |
  |---|---|---|
  | 8 | 0.9189 / 3.576 | 0.9156 / 3.576 |

  - At 8 parallel requests most steps have 8 rows, so both arms run the loop. At 4, every step takes the fused launches.

- Unchanged: runs without `--monokernel`, other archs and every existing fallback (greedy drafts, AV, more than 8 requests, ...). No new flag or env var. No CI config change.
- gfx942 is unchanged by construction: there `monokernel_cdna3_or_4()` is true exactly when `enabled()` is, and the step kernel keeps 4 warps and `MAX_ROWS` = 8. Nothing was rerun on gfx942.

## Details

**Why:** #60731 was the gfx942 port, so it gated all of its helpers on `ops.enabled()`, which is gfx942-only. `fused_markov` is plain Triton on vLLM's own Gumbel helpers, with nothing gfx942-specific.

**Change:**
- `fused_markov` runs under `ops.monokernel_cdna3_or_4()`: `--monokernel` + ROCm + `get_cdna_version() in (3, 4)`, the same CDNA versions as the mono layer.
  - `swa_decode_meta` (#63885) uses the same check, in place of its `swa_decode_meta_enabled()`.
  - The other helpers keep `ops.enabled()`.
- `_why_not` caps CDNA4 (gfx950) at `MAX_ROWS_GFX950` = 5 rows. A larger step takes vLLM's loop, as a step of more than `MAX_ROWS` = 8 already does on gfx942. Under FULL CUDA graphs, the choice is made at capture for each padded batch size.
- Both archs launch the step kernel at 4 warps. `BLOCK` is unchanged.
- No speculator, model-runner or CPU-GPU sync changes.
- Tests:
  - `test_dspark_draft_model_samples_with_fused_markov` now drives the real gate with stub kernels in 4 cases. With `--monokernel`, the fused launches take a 5-row step on gfx950 and an 8-row step on gfx942. vLLM's loop takes a 6-row step on gfx950, and any step without the flag.
  - The gfx942 kernel test clears the new check's cache.
  - No new test or test file.

**CI coverage:** there is no `.buildkite` change.
- The gate test runs in the existing MI355 "Basic Models (Other)" job.
- The kernel test runs only in the MI300 gfx942 kernels job.
- No CI job runs the fused launch on gfx950. It was run by hand at this head: 6 passed.

**Limitations:**
- The kernel test compares the fused launches with vLLM's loop at 1 and 2 requests and k=5 only.
- 3-5-row steps on gfx950 were timed by the microbench and ran end to end at c4 and in GSM8K. Outputs at those row counts are compared only through GSM8K and AL.
- AV on: no effect, because a step with the acceptance estimator or the confidence head falls back to the loop.
- `use_fp64_gumbel` is untested on either arch, as before this PR.

**Related PRs:**
- **#63181:** a separate fusion of the same loop: a Markov GEMV and a sample epilogue, 2 launches per draft position.
  - With both merged, this hook runs first. So #63181's path runs without `--monokernel`, on steps that `fused_markov` declines, and on gfx950 steps of 6-8 requests.
  - Its head predates `main`'s `DraftLogitsCache` and conflicts with `main`. The head-to-head waits for its rebase.
- **#63885** (merged: SWA decode metadata in one launch on gfx950) added `swa_decode_meta_enabled()`. This PR replaces it with `monokernel_cdna3_or_4()`, which both helpers use.
- **#63486** and **#61311** move or delete the test files and the MI300 CI job that runs them, and make the kernel test gfx942-only.
  - #61311 also renames `ops._monokernel` and `record_monokernel` to `_specialized_decode` and `record_specialized_decode`. The shared check and both tests use those names.
  - Whichever lands first, this PR follows: it widens that skip to gfx950 and, after #61311, renames the flag.
- **#61869:** a CUDA-only fused Markov sampler. It does not reach ROCm.
