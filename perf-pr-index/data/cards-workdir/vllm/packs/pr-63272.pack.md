## Overview

On ROCm, `mxfp4_hadamard32` layers on AITER's ASM MXFP4 GEMM now rotate their input inside the activation quant ([ROCm/aiter#6734](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/6734)) instead of in a separate PyTorch kernel, which recovers the 6–12% serving throughput that #62750's rotation costs on Qwen3.8-2.4T.

Not a duplicate: searched open PRs for "hadamard mxfp4" and "hadamard rotation aiter"; #65398 adds a MoE variant of the online scheme and does not fuse the rotation.

## Claims

- TP8 serving output throughput is within 0.6% of plain RTN `mxfp4` at concurrency 4, 64 and 256. With #62750's PyTorch rotation it is 6.0–12.4% lower.
- Per layer, the fused rotation adds 0–5% to quant + GEMM, where the PyTorch rotation adds 70–170%.
- The output matches the PyTorch rotation up to bf16 rounding, so #62750's accuracy results carry over.

## Validation

8x MI355X, Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B Quark MXFP4, TP8. vLLM nightly `df3bd094` plus the arm's diff, with ROCm/aiter#6734 ported to its AITER 0.1.25. All arms put the GDN projections (`in_proj_qkvz`, `out_proj`) and the shared experts on online MXFP4 (322 layers) and differ only in the GDN projections' scheme.

## Details

- `InputHadamardFusable` (in `input_hadamard.py`) is an opt-in mixin with `fuse_input_hadamard(layer, size) -> bool`. `InputHadamardLinearMethod` still rotates the weight, and skips its input rotation when the wrapped method is fusable and returns `True`. Online MXFP4, `QuarkLinearMethod` and Quark OCP MX inherit it and forward the call to their `MxFp4LinearKernel`.
- `AiterMxfp4LinearKernel` fuses only on its ASM GEMM path with size 32, and passes `hadamard_rotate=True` to the HIP quant. The Triton path keeps the PyTorch rotation. The server logs `... rotates its input by the 32-wide Hadamard matrix itself.` once per method.
- The ASM path is used only for (N, K) in AITER's tuned a4w4 config. `out_proj` is tuned today; ROCm/aiter#7441 adds the `in_proj_qkvz` (N=4608, K=8192) config that the numbers above include.
- Also fixes the fake impl of `gemm_with_dynamic_quant`, whose signature had drifted from the op.

**Limitations and CI gaps.** Serving used dummy weights, and accuracy was measured on #62750's unfused path; the fused path's equivalence rests on the per-element comparison above. No CI job runs these tests until the Quantization jobs are re-enabled (#66900, #66672). The MI355 job will then cover the fused path: the AITER cases turn AITER on themselves, since CI does not set `VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER`.
