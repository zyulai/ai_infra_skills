## 正文(PR 描述)

## Motivation

hit count will incorrect increase in the following situations, which will cause hiradix cache to offload a large amount of worthless cache, leading to increased costs.
- chunked_req, When a long input req is split into several chunks, each chunk will call `cache_unfinished_req` after prefill is completed, and the hit count will be incorrectly increased chunks time.
- When enable_hicache_storage is turned on, req will call `init_next_round_input` twice, and the hit count will be incorrectly increased
- req's hit count will increase in both _match_prefix_helper and _insert_helper, maybe just once is enough?
