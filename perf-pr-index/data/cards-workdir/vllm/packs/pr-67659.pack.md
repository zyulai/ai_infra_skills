## Overview

Apply sequence parallelism to HY4 iHC and normalization to eliminate redundant computation across TP ranks.

Keep iHC residuals token-sharded across decoder layers, using all-gather before attention and MLP and reduce-scatter after their outputs, without changing the existing attention or MoE parallel layout.

Reuse the sequence parallel infrastructure introduced in #66549. The model selects iHC SP on its own eligibility and token threshold, independently of attention ShardQ.

## Claims

- On 8×H200, TP8/DP1/EP8 prefill throughput improves **15.04% / 27.18% / 26.84%** at ISL2048 / 8192 / 32768, with median TTFT **−12.06% / −21.40% / −20.99%**, OSL1 and request-rate infinity.
- At an 8192-token step, each rank processes 1024 target iHC rows. Profiled iHC GPU time falls from **193.600 to 24.366 ms** per step, and the GPU busy interval from **729.533 to 571.312 ms**.
- Preserve global hidden-state rows for MTP and validate odd padding, shared indexers and prefix reuse.

Default breakable CUDA graphs and serving sequence limits, `max_model_len=262144`. ## Details

The local iHC stream owns `ceil(tokens / TP)` rows. Each subblock gathers the normalized hidden input, computes the existing attention/FFN path, and reduce-scatters the partial output before local post-gating. Final normalization is followed by a gather for MTP. Short model batches use the deferred all-reduce with replicated rows. The MoE constructor asserts that the factory honored deferred output reduction through `skip_final_all_reduce`.

Eligibility requires iHC, TP>1, DP1/PP1/PCP1/DCP1 and a step of at least 1024 model token rows. It does not depend on attention ShardQ, since attention and MLP always receive the gathered rows. LoRA, batch-invariant mode, auxiliary-hidden-state speculation and passthrough MoE are excluded. Humming is the measured MoE backend.

iHC SP also applies where ShardQ is disabled or ineligible. The TP2 regression covers that case, and its throughput has not been measured. Validation here covers H200 with FP8 KV. Blackwell and NVFP4-KV validation are pending.

The area search found no separate open HY4 iHC-SP implementation. #66549 provides the shared scheduling pattern, while #67490 enables sink-aware ShardQ in the measured configuration.

AI assistance was used for implementation, validation, review and description preparation.
