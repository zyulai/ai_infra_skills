## Overview

On SM107, the GLM low-latency GEMM table (`glm52_low_latency_gemm.py`) picks a backend per projection and token count M. This PR adds FlashInfer's warp split-K BF16 GEMM to it, next to the CuTe skinny GEMM, `dsv3_fused_a` and cuBLAS. It picks a backend by per-call timing for every M from 1 to 32 of each TP4 and replicated projection. Of the 224 (projection, M) points:
- warp split-K serves 199;
- the CuTe skinny GEMM serves 17: main's rows (#62843) at 15 points, all at M <= 12, plus two new indexer wk + weights_proj rows at M = 5 and 7;
- cuBLAS serves 8: the shared expert down at M 25-32.

Warp split-K compiles once per (N, K, tactic), with M dynamic. `_WarpSplitKGemm`, a `VllmJitKernel`, registers the planned compile keys with vLLM's JIT warmup, which compiles them before CUDA graph capture.

The PR also fixes q_b at M = 1 (`56fd4cb5a1`), whose table rows never ran in serving:
- **Cause:** `fused_norm_rope` allocated its q output with `torch.empty_like(q_c)`, and the attention layer passes `q_c` as a column slice of the fused q_a + kv_a output. At one token that slice counts as dense, so the output kept its row stride: 2624 for GLM-5.2/5.3, or 2784 on indexer layers when wk is gathered with it under sequence-parallel MoE.
- **Effect:** the GLM tables only take packed rows, so q_b_proj and the indexer's wq_b fell back to cuBLAS at M = 1 wherever their shape has an M = 1 row. That's q_b at TP8 on SM100, SM103 and SM107, and q_b at TP4 and wq_b on SM100 and SM107. All of these rows are main's, so the bug predates this PR.
- **Fix:** `fused_norm_rope` now allocates the q output packed. From two tokens on, `empty_like` already returned a packed tensor.

SM100's and SM103's tables are unchanged. On those GPUs only the q_b fix changes what runs, and it wasn't measured there. The MTP `eh_proj` rows apply now that #65704 is merged; before it, GLM's MTP layer never built its `eh_proj` plan and ran cuBLAS.

## Claims

- **Kernels, per call:** each call is timed alone in a CUDA graph, after a sleep kernel whose time is subtracted, so no GEMM overlaps another. Weights are cold in L2, and PDL is on where the kernel supports it. All runs are on VR200; Validation lists them.
  - Over the 216 planned points, the table is 1.21x faster than cuBLAS (geomean; 1.01-2.10x per point).
  - Over the 201 points where it differs from main's table, it is 1.16x faster than main's row (geomean; 0.99-1.63x per point). The one point below 1.00, q_a + kv_a at M = 2, is within the 1-2% run-to-run noise.
- **Serving:** GLM-5.3-NVFP4, TP4 on one VR NVL72 tray (4x VR200), MTP K7 with synthetic acceptance length 3.91, 8k in / 1k out. Measured in two steps on two nodes (see Validation):
    - +6.9% / +6.8% / +1.8% output throughput at c1 / c2 / c4 (c1 median TPOT 2.27 -> 2.11 ms), and within noise from c8.
    - The rows cover M <= 32: with K7, the target pass (M = 8c) up to c4 and the draft decode steps (M = c) up to c32.
    - On SM107, #65704 alone only moves the MTP `eh_proj` at M 1-4 from cuBLAS to main's CuTe rows. An earlier revision of this PR alone (`fc0647f10a`) gave +6.0% at c1 and +2.2% at c4, from one launch per side on an older tree with #65704 on both sides.
  - **`7f07ac6556` against `7f2d731097` (this PR minus its last two commits):**
    - -0.13% / +0.21% / +0.19% / -0.09% at c1 / c2 / c4 / c8, inside the 1.06% launch-to-launch spread.
    - They differ in the 29 rows that used vLLM's `ll_bf16` GEMM. `7f2d731097` serves them with warp split-K, main's CuTe rows, or cuBLAS at wk + weights_proj M = 7.
  - **Not served:** `87879f6f37` (wk + weights_proj M = 5 and 7) and the q_b fix.
    - With K7, wk runs at 8 tokens per request, so it never sees M = 5 or 7.

- **Numerics:** errors are on par with cuBLAS BF16. Over the 216 planned points, the worst relative L2 error against an FP32 reference is 1.76e-3, and every point's error is within 0.001% of cuBLAS's. GSM8K is unchanged at c1 and c4 (earlier revision; see Validation).
- **q_b at M = 1 (`56fd4cb5a1`):** on VR200 (SM107), q_b_proj at TP4 (4096 x 2048) and M = 1 now runs the table's CuTe kernel instead of cuBLAS.
  - 5.02 -> 3.37 us per call isolated (1.49x), and 4.13 -> 2.98 us back to back (1.39x).
  - It shows up at batch 1 without MTP, in every layer's q_b_proj and every indexer layer's wq_b. With MTP, it shows up only in the MTP layer's q_b_proj, during its one-token draft steps (which skip the indexer) while one request is running.
  - The fix's other M = 1 rows weren't measured: q_b at TP8 (SM100, SM103, SM107) and SM100's q_b TP4 / wq_b row.

## Details

- **Why call `run_warp_splitk_dense` directly instead of `flashinfer.mm_bf16` (or `--linear-backend flashinfer_cutedsl`):**
  - `mm_bf16` takes no tactic, so it runs FlashInfer's default tactic or its autotuner. The table pins the tactic measured for each (N, K) at the swept M.
  - The warmup compiles each planned (N, K, tactic) through FlashInfer's `_compile`, with the arguments `run_warp_splitk_dense(pdl=True)` passes. `_compile` is private, so the SM107 test above checks that the warmup and the calls hit the same cache entries. The SM107 tests ran with 0.7.2.post1; the arguments also match the 0.7.3 and 0.7.4 sources.
  - Since #66030 (merged 2026-11-06), main also has an adapted copy of this kernel for Kimi K3.1's specialized decode, in `vllm/models/kimi_k3/nvidia/specialized_decode/cutedsl/gemm/warp_splitk.py`. It has the same five tactic fields; this PR calls FlashInfer's module.
- **Why no `ll_bf16`:**
  - At wk + weights_proj M 9-11 and 13-32, where warp split-K replaced it, vLLM's `ll_bf16_gemm` was 1.12-1.17x faster per isolated call (0.40-0.54 us).
  - wk runs about 22 times per decode step (21 indexer layers plus the MTP draft's first step), so that gap is under 0.1% of a 10-13 ms step at c2-c4, and the serving A/B showed no difference.
  - At gate_up M 1-2, main's CuTe rows are within 1% of `ll_bf16`.
  - So this revision's only new backend is warp split-K. Its CuTe rows are main's, except the two new wk rows at M = 5 and 7.
- **Why fix `fused_norm_rope`, not the tables' layout check:** a freshly allocated output has no reason to inherit its input's row stride. Packing it is one line, costs nothing (2048 elements either way) and fixes q_b_proj and wq_b at once.
  - `.contiguous()` wouldn't help, because PyTorch counts a one-row tensor as contiguous whatever its row stride.
  - Relaxing `_runtime_ok` to `row_stride_ok` for CuTe rows, as #60027 did for Qwen4Exp, would also work. Fixing `fused_norm_rope` instead keeps the tables' packed-only contract. Main pins that contract with `test_glm52_layout_rejects_nonpacked_single_row_view`, and with `test_glm52_q_b_nonpacked_single_row_falls_back` for exactly this q_b input.
  - `fused_norm_rope` is common code, so DeepSeek-V3.2 and the ROCm attention path also get a packed q at one token. Values don't change, and nothing reads the old stride.
- **Warp split-K is not bit-exact with the backends it replaces (cuBLAS, CuTe, `dsv3_fused_a`):** it sums in a different order, so greedy text can drift from stock. The q_b fix likewise moves q_b and wq_b at M = 1 from cuBLAS to main's CuTe rows, on SM100 and SM103 too. Errors are on par with cuBLAS BF16 (see Claims). Accuracy at c1 and c4, where these rows carry the target pass, was unchanged with an earlier revision (see Validation).
- **PDL:** `pdl=True` is hard-coded for warp split-K.
  - flashinfer-ai/flashinfer#8081 (open) fixes memory-ordering gaps in this kernel, including output stores under PDL. That PR notes that these gaps change no result in replay tests, so our passing tests and serving runs do not rule them out.
  - Its fix keeps outputs bitwise identical and needs no change here. It takes effect once it merges and vLLM's FlashInfer pin (0.7.4 on main, 0.7.3 at this PR's base; neither includes it) moves to a release that does.
- **Timing method:** revisions before `7f07ac6556` timed each kernel back to back, so under PDL each call overlapped the next. That flatters warp split-K, which streams its weights before the PDL wait. The rows are picked from isolated timings.
- **Scope and fallback:**
  - 177 added and 55 removed lines outside tests.
  - The replicated projections (q_a + kv_a, indexer wq_b and wk + weights_proj) and `eh_proj` keep their shapes under TP8 and DEP on SM107, so these rows apply there too. Only TP4 was measured end to end.
  - If FlashInfer's warp split-K module cannot be imported, those rows fall back to cuBLAS.
- **AI assistance:** This PR was developed with AI assistance, which also ran the tests and measurements above on our VR200 nodes. I reviewed every changed line.
