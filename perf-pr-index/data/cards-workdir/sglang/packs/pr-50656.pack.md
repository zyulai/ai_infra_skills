## Motivation

Follow-up to #49205 (hier_2shot_push), #49955 (ring_simple and unrolled 2shot_push) and #50660 (hier_ring_simple), on current `main`. Everything here is on the gfx1250 path.

On gfx1250, every 2shot_push up to 4 MB that doesn't take the unrolled kernel is clamped to 256 threads per block:

```cpp
if (two_shot && is_gfx1250() && nbytes <= int64_t{4} << 20) block_size = 256u;
```

The comment says this helps when ranks arrive skewed. On the current kernels it doesn't: with one rank ~20 us late, the default block size is as fast or faster at every size, with host and device kernel arguments alike. Back to back, the clamp is expensive. Forced 2shot_push, graph mode:

| | size | clamp | no clamp | late rank |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TP2 | 4 MB | 19.9 us | 9.1 us (-54%) | -29% |
| TP4 | 1 MB | 23.6 us | 12.7 us (-46%) | -21% |
| TP4 | 4 MB | 80.2 us | 30.6 us (-62%) | -55% |
| TP6 | 1 MB | 27.9 us | 17.7 us (-36%) | not run |
| TP8 | 512 KB | 18.6 us | 14.1 us (-24%) | -12% |

The gfx1250 cutoffs were picked with the clamp in place, so several of them no longer sit at the crossovers. #49955 also didn't tune TP16 ("the TP16 hier path (two hosts) has not been rerun on this stack"). This PR removes the clamp, retunes TP2-TP16 against the new kernels, gives the DPX pair stages of hier_2shot_push an unrolled kernel, and extends hier_ring_simple (#50660) to TP16.

## Modifications

- `custom_all_reduce.cuh`:
  - drop the gfx1250 256-thread clamp for 2shot_push;
  - let the unrolled 2shot_push run at world size 16 (was ≤ 8). It gives every peer a warp, so a grid with fewer warps than peers (a one-block grid at TP16) takes the per-vector kernel instead.
- `two_shot_push_unrolled.cuh`: unrolled 2shot_push thresholds.
  - TP4 from 2 MB (was 8 MB). Without the clamp it wins from 2 MB: 15% back to back, 10% with a late rank. The old comment about late ranks favoring the per-vector kernel up to 4 MB no longer holds.
  - TP16 at every size. It is 19-48% faster than the per-vector kernel from 64 KB to 64 MB, back to back and with a late rank.
  - TP2/TP3 stay at 8 MB; TP5-TP8 are unchanged.
- `dpx_pair_push.cuh`: `pair_push_unrolled_kernel` for the hier pair reduce-scatter/all-gather, on the unrolled 2shot_push pipeline:
  - warps take whole hunks, issue every load before using any, and send batched system-scope stores;
  - a late peer is re-polled one hunk at a time;
  - the tail takes the per-vector path.

  Marker handling, slot reset, epoch commit and PDL placement match `pair_push_kernel`. It takes chunks of 2 MB and up; below that the per-vector kernel is 7-19% faster.
- `configs/custom_all_reduce_v2.py`:

Adding one of each, 512 KB comes out 2.5 us faster per layer, 1 MB 3 us and 2 MB 8 us. 2shot_push keeps its 64 MB threshold, so in the host-kernarg table, with the default 16 MB communicator cap (hier up to 32 MB), 32-64 MB stays on 2shot_push as before, now 7-12% faster.
