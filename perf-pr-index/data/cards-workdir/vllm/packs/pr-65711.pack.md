## Overview

Routes per-tensor FP8 linear layers (static activation scales) to AITER's Triton `gemm_a8w8` for decode batches of 5 to 256 tokens, on shapes AITER has tuned configs for. Everything else keeps the current `ROCmFP8ScaledMMLinearKernel` path.

Requires [ROCm/aiter#7347](https://github.com/ROCm/aiter/pull/7347) which was released in v0.1.28.post1.

## Claims

Llama 3.1 8B FP8 on MI355X (gfx950):

- **Decode is faster:** at 1k/1k, concurrency 16–256, TPOT is 1.3–8.1% lower and total token throughput is 1.3–8.4% higher. At 8k/1k, concurrency 16 has 6.1% lower TPOT and 9.5% higher throughput; concurrency 32–256 moves the same way but within run-to-run noise.
- **TTFT is unchanged or better at concurrency 16–256.** At concurrency 4, where Triton is not used, TTFT is 1.4 ms (+2.6%) higher at 1k and 5.1 ms (+1.2%) higher at 8k. Both are under 0.2% of request latency, and throughput is unchanged.
- **Accuracy is unchanged:** GSM8K and GPQA-diamond within noise in every cell.

## Details

- Every AITER FP8 kernel on ROCm needs per-token scales, so per-tensor checkpoints always used hipBLASLt for decode. Tuned Triton `gemm_a8w8` is faster there.
- The new kernel subclasses `ROCmFP8ScaledMMLinearKernel`. The batch-size check is inside the custom op, so batches outside 5–256 fall back to the existing path without torch.compile specializing on it.
- Selection is gated on AITER tuned configs, following [#61278](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/61278). No new environment variables. FNUZ (gfx942) is excluded.
- Not a duplicate: no open PR routes per-tensor FP8 to `gemm_a8w8`.
- AI assistance was used. I reviewed every changed line and ran the tests and benchmarks above.
