## Motivation

`DYNAMIC_CUDNN_SDPA` sends every dense non-causal call on sm_10x to cuDNN SDPA, a choice measured on B200 ("cuDNN SDPA measured 1.25-1.5x faster than FA4 CuTe"). On B300 (sm_103) FA4 uses the hardware exp2 path and the `tcgen05.ld.red` row max, and long video self-attention is where diffusion models spend most of their step (Wan2.2 A14B 720p: ~67% of a denoising step).

A video DiT on B300 runs at the 1100 W power cap for the whole denoise, so kernels were timed under sustained load (2 s warm-up to the cap, then 2-6 s of back-to-back calls), bf16, B=1 unless noted:

FA4's sm_103 tuning knobs (softmax/correction registers, exp2 emulation, row-max reduction, 2-CTA) were swept at the Wan shape; the defaults are the best of them.

## Modifications

`layers/attention/backends/sdpa.py`: on sm_103, `DynamicCudnnSDPAImpl` takes FA4 for self-attention (`Sq == Skv`) with head_dim 128 and at least 16384 tokens. B200 (sm_100), cross-attention, shorter sequences and head_dim 64 keep cuDNN.

Both are exact softmax attention in bf16 with fp32 accumulation; only the rounding order changes (max |diff| 2.4e-4 on unit-scale inputs).

This touches the same function as "Route dense attention with a sub-tile side to FA4 on Blackwell"; the two conditions are independent and rebase trivially.

## Accuracy Tests

- `test_dynamic_cudnn_sdpa_selection.py`: sm_103 routes 16K and 75.6K self-attention to FA4; 8K, 512-key cross-attention and head_dim 64 stay on cuDNN; sm_100 stays on cuDNN at 75.6K.
- Wan2.2 T2V A14B 720p, 4xB300, same prompts and seeds, video at x264 crf 0, against cuDNN (with the other, bit-identical B300 Wan changes and a 63 dB VAE change in the build): PSNR 27.5 / 22.4 dB, SSIM 0.989 / 0.965 on two prompts. Same scene and composition; the 40-step trajectories drift apart as with any change of rounding order (the subject's pose differs by the last frame).

## Speed Tests and Profiling

Wan2.2 T2V A14B 720p, 81 frames, 40 steps, 4xB300, CFG 2 x Ulysses 2, eager. Attention per block (5 head groups of 4 heads): 38.1 -> 36.8 ms. E2E with the Ulysses pipeline fix: main 92.83 / 92.61 s -> 91.23-91.44 s (6 requests, median 91.34 s). On an older main with NCCL all-to-all, `--attention-backend fa` measured 95.75 s against 97.08 s for the default selection.
