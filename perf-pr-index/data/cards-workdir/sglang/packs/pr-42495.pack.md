## Motivation

`qwen_image_t2i_cache_dit_enabled` fails its H100 per-step perf check at random. From 09-30 to 10-04, 71% of attempts went over the step-49 limit. Every final 1-GPU failure in that window came from this case.

The cause, found in the debug runs in #42546, is how the step time is measured. It is not a regression.

- Steps 10 and 49 are the first cached step after a full step.
- Per-step times are host wall times with no device sync.
- The cache-dit residual check in that step is the first point where the host blocks, so that step absorbs the previous full step's queued GPU work.

Debug run on CI H100, 3 launches per arm, times in ms:

| Arm | Full step | Steps 10 / 49 | Other cached steps | E2E |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Fused QK-norm/RoPE epilogue on, unsynced (CI today) | 178 | 64 / 66 | 6.3 | 4310 |
| Fused off, unsynced | 196 | 55 / 56 | 6.5 | 4455 |
| Fused on, `SGLANG_DIFFUSION_SYNC_STAGE_PROFILING=1` | 238 | 7.0 / 7.1 | 6.3 | — |
| Fused off, synced | 245 | 7.2 / 7.1 | 6.5 | — |

With synced timing, steps 10 and 49 take about 7 ms, like every other cached step. #38584 enabled the fused epilogue on Hopper, which cut host launches from 3031 to 2007 per profiled window. The real GPU time got about 3% faster. But the host now enqueues faster too, so more GPU work spills into steps 10 and 49.

## Modifications

`perf_baselines/h100.json`: steps 10 and 49 of `qwen_image_t2i_cache_dit_enabled` are set to their CI medians, 64.58 / 66.38 ms (previously 48.7 / 45.11). The medians come from 328 H100 CI measurements between 09-30 and 10-04. They are the unsynced host wall times this check measures. No other baseline or tolerance changes.

The `ltx_2_5_diffusion_decoder_2gpus` timing tolerance from earlier commits is dropped. Its host-bound slowdown will be handled in a separate PR.

## Validation

PR CI on fe3b48d passed with the same qwen baselines.
