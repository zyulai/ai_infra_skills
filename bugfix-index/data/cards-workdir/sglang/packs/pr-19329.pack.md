## Motivation

fix symm not enabled due to incorrect registration of comm.

1. `get_local_dp_buffer` uses the group obtained by `get_tp_group` by default to register symm.  
2. The `attn_cp_all_gather_into_tensor` operation uses the group obtained by `get_attention_cp_group` to perform allgather.  
3. Steps 1 and 2 cause symm to fail when enabled because the two groups are inconsistent.

https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/671b59557045f13c13650c29b4e56a02874f7fcd/python/sglang/srt/layers/communicator_nsa_cp.py#L141-L163

https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/c4ef33862b30c6c41eeaefd1fdd4739243a838a6/python/sglang/srt/layers/dp_attention.py#L129-L130

https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/c4ef33862b30c6c41eeaefd1fdd4739243a838a6/python/sglang/srt/layers/dp_attention.py#L572-L573

https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/671b59557045f13c13650c29b4e56a02874f7fcd/python/sglang/srt/distributed/device_communicators/pynccl_allocator.py#L180-L184

https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/c4ef33862b30c6c41eeaefd1fdd4739243a838a6/python/sglang/srt/distributed/device_communicators/pynccl_allocator.py#L52-L58


## Modifications

Modify the functions `get_local_dp_buffer` and `get_global_dp_buffer` to add the `GroupOrdinator` parameter, which can make the comm group registered by symm consistent with the comm group of the collection operator.
