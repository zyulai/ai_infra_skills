## Modifications

In the current implementation, `padded_scales` is initialized with shape `(B, M_padded, K_padded)`, but later reshaped to `(B, M, K)` or `(M, K)` depending on `scale_ndim`. This leads to a mismatch and potential runtime errors or incorrect memory alignment, especially during NVFP4 quantized inference using ModelOpt in SGLang.
