## Overview

On gfx950, `RocmDotScaledMxfp8LinearKernel` (1x32-scale MXFP8 dense layers: MiniMax-M3/M3.1, ModelOpt/INC MXFP8) runs a separate `mxfp8_e4m3_quantize` launch before every `tl.dot_scaled` GEMM. For bf16 activations with 1-16 rows, this PR quantizes the activation inside the GEMM instead, so each such linear is one launch and the output is bit-identical. Fused: K <= 1024 for M <= 16, and K = 2048 for M <= 4.

The fused GEMM quantizes only `min(16, next_pow2(M))` activation rows per K step and repeats them to fill the 16-row MFMA tile; that keeps the per-N-tile quant cost proportional to the real rows and is what makes K = 2048 profitable.

## Claims

- Per-call speedup on MI355X: 1.14x-1.64x for K <= 1024 at M = 1-16, and 1.03x-1.27x for K = 2048 at M = 1-4 (1.15x-1.27x for MiniMax-M3.1 o_proj at TP4, 6144 x 2048). Every other shape and M keeps the existing path (1.00x).
- Output is bitwise equal to the existing path (separate quant + `_select_cfg` tile), including where the fused tile has a different `BLOCK_K`/`BLOCK_N`.
- MiniMax-M3.1-Flash TP4 (on #63232), AgentX concurrency 1: o_proj and shared-expert down_proj run fused with no separate quant launch. Over baseline, in two runs with the arm order swapped: P90 TPOT 1.011x in both, P90 interactivity 1.011-1.012x.
- No change under `VLLM_BATCH_INVARIANT`, for fp16 activations, for M > 16, for K = 2048 at M > 4, or for K > 2048.

## Details

- **Row trimming.** `BLOCK_M` = 16 is the smallest MFMA tile, but at M < 16 only the first M rows are stored. The kernel takes a constexpr `QUANT_ROWS = min(16, next_pow2(M))`, loads and quantizes that many rows, and repeats them with `broadcast_to` + `reshape`. At `BLOCK_K` = 128 the repeat costs more than the saved quant, so those tiles (K = 384, 640, ...) keep quantizing 16 rows. The unfused path passes `QUANT_ROWS = BLOCK_M` and generates the same code as before.
- **Why only M <= 16, and K = 2048 only up to M = 4.** The fused GEMM requantizes the activation once per N-tile. At small M the separate launch (plus the round trip of the fp8 activation and its scales) dominates; with more rows or larger K the redundant quant work wins (table above). The gate does not look at N; cutting K = 2048 at M = 4 keeps every measured N at or above 1.03x.
- **`K` becomes a `tl.constexpr`.** Without it the fused loop is not unrolled/pipelined and the fused GEMM is ~0.8x even at K = 512. #63232 makes the same change.
- **Compiles.** Triton already specializes M on `== 1` and `% 16 == 0`; `QUANT_ROWS` adds up to three more variants per fused shape (6 instead of 3 for M = 1-16 at `BLOCK_K` >= 256; 5 at K = 2048, where M = 5-16 stays unfused). For MiniMax-M3.1 at TP4 that is 17 instead of 12 `_mxfp8_linear_kernel` compiles over M = 1-16 (15 instead of 12 at TP8). They are compiled in the eager warm-up before CUDA graph capture; vLLM's capture sizes include every M from 1 to 16, so decode never compiles at runtime, and graph capture time was unchanged in the end-to-end runs.
- **bf16 only.** The in-kernel fp8 cast makes the compiler enable the FP16 overflow clamp in the MODE register for the whole kernel, so an fp16 output that overflows saturates at +-65504 where the unfused GEMM produces inf. bf16 output is unaffected and bit-identical; fp16 activations keep the separate quant.
- **Batch invariance.** Fused and unfused use different tiles (and therefore K-reduction orders), so under `VLLM_BATCH_INVARIANT` (#52231) every M stays on the unfused path.
- **Limitations.** Only gfx950 (`dot_scaled` path). Shapes are tuned on MI355X.
