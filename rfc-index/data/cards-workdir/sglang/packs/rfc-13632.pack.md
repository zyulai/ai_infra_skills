## 正文(PRD / 提案全文)

As mentioned at [discussion](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/discussions/13579).

I suggest that we can keep centralized issue tracking at `/sglang`, but move `sgl-kernel` and `sgl-router` into their own dedicated repositories.

This provides a much cleaner source code view, eliminating unrelated but crossed commit history. More importantly, it will enable independent and faster CI/CD pipelines, allowing contributors to focus exclusively on their specific modules.

It definitely will reduce cognitive load for SGL developers/maintainers and users.
